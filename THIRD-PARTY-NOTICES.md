Drift bundles Electron/Chromium, React, Lucide, electron-store, Ghostery's adblocker and tldts, along with their dependencies. Package license files remain in the packaged dependency tree. Electron's LICENSE and Chromium's LICENSES.chromium.html ship beside the application executable. Review these notices before redistributing a modified build.

EasyList and EasyPrivacy are bundled under assets/filters, retaining their original copyright and licensing headers. See assets/filters/README.md for their official sources. The complete bundled engine also includes uBlock Origin's filter lists and scriptlet resources (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uAssets) and Peter Lowe's ad server list from Ghostery's adblocker asset mirror. Unmodified source bodies, source URLs, checksums and original copyright notices are retained in assets/filters/full-sources.json and full-engine.json; the GPL license is in UBLOCK-LICENSE.txt. The same sources supply runtime updates. Ghostery's adblocker is licensed under MPL-2.0; its installed package includes its license and source references. The application does not modify that dependency.

The browser does not bundle Chrome, Brave, Zen, Comet, Perplexity, Google or DuckDuckGo logos or proprietary assets. Website favicons and content are retrieved from user-visited pages.
